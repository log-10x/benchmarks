A handful of messages quote dates from outside the capture, build stamps and
the like, which is why the window is taken between percentiles rather than
between the extremes. This is one small demo cluster over a few hours, not a
log platform. A shape that occurs once here is a different population from a
shape that occurs once at a hundred thousand lines a second, and the frequency
strata in results/identity.md should be read against this scale.

The message is the `log` field of each Fluentd/Kubernetes record. The rest
is envelope, identical in shape on every line, so a miner fed whole lines
spends most of its similarity budget on the envelope. Every script here
feeds the extracted message.
